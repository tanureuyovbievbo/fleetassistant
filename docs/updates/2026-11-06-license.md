## Why Apache-2.0 and not MIT

MIT is one paragraph and matches HAEO, but it grants no patent licence and has no
contribution clause. Apache-2.0 §3 (patent grant with retaliation) and §5
(inbound=outbound contributions) are the clauses a utility procurement review or
an acquirer actually checks. Both licences leave the hosted fleet closed; AGPL-3.0
would not have.
